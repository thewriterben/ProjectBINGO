# Project BINGO × Digital Gold: Supply-Chain Onboarding, Made Operational

**A proposal to the Digital Gold Foundation**
*From: Benjamin J. Snider — August 2026 — Draft for discussion with John and the Foundation*

---

## The one-paragraph version

The White Paper is unambiguous about what makes a coin money: not the consumer→merchant hop, but circulation through the chain behind it — merchant to wholesaler to manufacturer to supplier. It names supply-chain onboarding as the fourth participation pathway, "the patient multi-year work of penetrating real-economy supply chains link by link," and calls that circulation the operational mechanism behind every other metric. Project BINGO is that pathway, built and running: an open manufacturing network where a buyer's single payment settles atomically across an entire supply chain — fabricator, designer, logistics, quality inspection — in one transaction. Last night it executed its first real fabrication on physical hardware, with every link paid at the moment of verified delivery. It is a supply chain in a settlement, and it is the natural place for Digital Gold to demonstrate Adequate Circulation not as a pillar on a page, but as a working economy.

## What BINGO is

An open protocol (specs and code public, MIT) connecting three things: designers who register functional designs with per-unit royalties enforced at the point of fabrication; a network of independent printers and machine shops that fabricate with signed, hash-chained proof-of-fabrication evidence; and settlement that releases escrow through every participant's share in one atomic transaction. No token of its own — by design, and permanently. Settlement is denominated in dollars with regulated rails as the on-ramp, and the settlement layer was architected from day one around the DGD escrow model: non-custodial, partial-release, oracle-verified.

Status, honestly stated: working prototype. First physical unit fabricated and settled August 3 on a Creality K2 Plus — design registered, royalty split frozen, telemetry signed into evidence, atomic settlement on receipt ($0.40 designer royalty, $1.48 fabrication, network fee, escrow zeroed to the cent). One node, closed loop, all parties me. The next gate is strangers transacting, via a pilot in the Meshtastic open-hardware community (10 designers, 10 printer nodes). Full architecture, market evidence, and milestone-gated plan: VISION.md, LANDSCAPE-2026.md, and RESOURCING.md in the repository.

## Why this serves the framework — in its own terms

**Adequate Circulation, demonstrated.** The lumber-yard example in §10 describes exactly what BINGO's settlement does mechanically. When a buyer funds an order, the release doesn't stop at a merchant: it flows to the node operator, the designer, the logistics pool, and — as the network matures — the filament supplier and the inspector, in one transaction. A DGD that enters BINGO doesn't need to convert out to be useful: the node operator's earnings are spendable inside the same network on materials, designs, and services. That is the merchant→wholesaler→manufacturer→supplier loop, compressed and verifiable. Every closed loop is the White Paper's definition of "become money in the operational sense" — auditable on a public ledger.

**The CFV effect-metrics, supplied.** The framework is candid that adoption drives the curve but that transactions, transaction value, and developer activity "will arise (or not) from actual use as money in commerce." BINGO manufactures precisely those three: every order is transactions and transaction value; an open protocol with public specs accretes developers. The Foundation cannot will commerce into existence; it can back a venue where commerce is the product.

**The cleanest acquisition channel that can exist.** BINGO participants don't buy anything — they *earn* through productive work: fabrication performed, designs licensed, inspections completed. Recognition of work rendered is commerce, not expectation of profit from others' efforts. Every node operator and designer who chooses DGD settlement becomes a confirmed participant who acquired the coin by making something. There is no cleaner story for a digital commodity, and no channel more consistent with the compliance discipline the ambassador program already enforces.

**The campus strategy, given hands.** The current grassroots plan meets students where they are; BINGO gives them something to *do*. Every engineering building and maker space in America is full of idle printers and students who need money. A campus BINGO node turns a dorm-room printer into income; a design student's bracket earns royalties from machines she'll never see; the club that needs fifty enclosures buys them from the network. "Sound money" is an argument; "the money your printer earned last night" is an experience. Ambassadors recruiting nodes and designers are onboarding supply-chain links — the fourth pathway — not just posting content. The two programs are one program.

## What is being asked, and what is not

**Not asked:** any protocol change (nothing to govern, nothing proposed); any purchase requirement (DGD settlement is optional in BINGO, always, with the founder relationship disclosed loudly in every public document — it already is); any exclusivity (BINGO's dollar rails remain the compliance-safe default for participants who want them).

**Asked, in sequence:**

1. **Alignment (costs nothing).** The Foundation acknowledges BINGO as a supply-chain onboarding effort consistent with the fourth pathway, and we jointly define what link-by-link recognition looks like for network participants who settle in DGD — announced, as always, through the Foundation's official channels.
2. **Pilot support (small, gated).** Ecosystem-grant-scale backing — on the order of $50K equivalent in deployable funds, not ticker math — to fund the Meshtastic pilot: hardware subsidies for first nodes, legal review of license templates, pilot operations. Released against the same gate the whole project answers to: verified transactions between strangers.
3. **Phase 1 consideration (only if the gate passes).** A staged path toward the $250–500K Phase 1 described in RESOURCING.md, at the Foundation's discretion, once the pilot has produced circulating, closed-loop settlement worth pointing at.
4. **Campus convergence (planning now, execution with the ambassador program).** One pilot campus where ambassador recruitment and node onboarding run together, instrumented so we learn whether "earnable money" outperforms "explained money" — my hypothesis is that it isn't close.

## What the Foundation gets to point at

Within two quarters, if gates pass: a public dashboard showing real goods manufactured across independent nodes, every settlement transparent, with DGD circulating through multi-link loops that never touch an exchange, a spread, or a speculative narrative — the anti-degen coin doing the most boring and most radical thing a currency can do: paying people for work, link by link. The framework says the ideal currency is proven. This is what realizing it looks like: not another argument that DGD is money, but a place where it undeniably *is*.
