# Meshtastic pilot — designer outreach

Started 2026-08-03 by the ops agent; decisions locked 2026-08-03 by Ben. Purpose: channel map + outreach copy for recruiting the 10 designers / 10 printer nodes. **Nothing is sent by an agent — Ben sends, personalized.**

## Decisions (locked)

1. **First channel: Cults3D designers first.** They already sell their files, so they're pre-selected for wanting to be paid — the least friction "yes." Printables second. Regional mesh group (e.g. NodakMesh) approached in parallel as a *node* cluster, not for designers.
2. **Royalty framing: creator sets their own price.** Do not pitch a platform percentage — that's the model everyone's fleeing. The pitch is "you set your royalty, you keep it, it's enforced automatically." When asked for a norm, anchor with "most land around 30–50¢ per unit fabricated" (the first real settlement was 40¢ on a ~$2 job). The point that closes: it's *per unit, forever, on top of* whatever they already earn elsewhere — not a replacement, an addition, and one no platform offers.
3. **Sequence:** designers first (Cults shortlist of 5), nodes via one regional mesh conversation, and hold any public Discord/forum post until 2–3 designers have said yes — social proof before broadcast.

## Channel map (where enclosure designers actually are)

- **Cults3D** — ~166 models under the meshtastic tag; designers already sell files → the most royalty-receptive pool. **Start here.**
- **Printables** — most active venue for current Meshtastic enclosure work (Heltec V3 cases, an 18650-UPS Heltec V3 enclosure in open beta — a designer iterating in public is a strong candidate). Free-first culture, so the royalty pitch is a new idea to them rather than an obvious yes. Second.
- **meshtastic.org community enclosures pages** — curated community designs by board (RAK WisBlock, Heltec, T-Beam) with named designers; good for identifying respected/canonical designs to approach.
- **Official meshtastic/enclosures GitHub repo** — dormant since late 2024; README redirects to Printables/Thingiverse. Skip; it just confirms where the energy went.
- **Meshtastic Discord + discourse forum** — main community channels; the place for a "we're piloting paid fabrication" post *after* 2–3 designers are aboard, not for cold recruiting.
- **Regional mesh groups (e.g. NodakMesh)** — regional communities curate enclosure lists and run hardware-heavy memberships; one group can supply multiple printer nodes in a single relationship. Pick 2–3 active regional meshes as concentrated node targets.

## First moves

1. Shortlist 5 Cults3D designers with recent (2025–26) Meshtastic uploads and visible activity (sales, comments, updates).
2. Approach 1 regional mesh group as a node cluster — one conversation, multiple printers.
3. Hold Discord/forum posting until 2–3 designers have said yes.

## Draft DM (designer, ~90 words — Ben personalizes and sends)

> Hi [name] — I came across your [design] on [platform]; it's one of the better-engineered Meshtastic enclosures out there. I'm running a small pilot of an open protocol where designers get paid a royalty automatically every time someone fabricates their design on a networked printer — you set your own royalty, you keep full ownership, and it's enforced at the point of fabrication instead of on the honor system. It's per unit, forever, on top of whatever you already earn. First real settlement already ran end-to-end on my own machine. Recruiting 10 designers for the pilot — want the details?

**If they ask "how much?":** "You set it. Most land around 30–50¢ per unit — the first one that ran was 40¢. It's paid to you the moment the part is made, automatically, on any printer in the network." (Never quote a percentage; creator-sets-price is the whole point.)

**If they ask "what's the catch / what do you take?":** "A flat 3% network fee on the job — for comparison, Printables and Cults take 20%. I'm not trying to be another platform; I'm building the rails."
