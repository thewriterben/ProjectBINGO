# Newerascontinue Materials - Index

## Overview

This directory contains optimized investor materials for the Global AI Marketplace (related to ProjectBINGO). These materials were created as part of the business development and fundraising efforts.

**Note**: While these materials reference a "Global AI Marketplace," they represent early ideation around business models and investor positioning that can inform the ProjectBINGO strategy.

## Differences from ProjectBINGO

While these materials focus on a "Global AI Marketplace," ProjectBINGO has a distinct focus:

| Aspect | Newerascontinue Materials | ProjectBINGO |
|--------|---------------------------|--------------|
| **Focus** | Global AI marketplace with crypto integration | AI-powered decentralized manufacturing marketplace |
| **Primary Users** | Crypto-native users, rural markets | Manufacturers, creators, designers, developers |
| **Core Value** | AI-driven pricing and marketplace efficiency | Manufacturing matching and supply chain transparency |
| **Technology** | AI + blockchain + general marketplace | AI + Web3 + manufacturing-specific tools |
| **Stage** | Investor pitch and fundraising materials | Research and planning phase |
